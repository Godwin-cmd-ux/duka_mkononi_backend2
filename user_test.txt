================================================================================
 DUKA MKONONI — USER TEST WORKFLOWS (steps only)
================================================================================


-----------------------------------
 LOGIN (do this first)
-----------------------------------
1. Open the Duka Mkononi website.
2. Log in with the Seller email and password.
3. You land on the Seller portal.
4. In the left menu, find the four items at the bottom:
     Ushauri wa Bei | Mpango wa Kuagiza | Uliza Biashara | Afya ya Biashara


================================================================================
 WORKFLOW 1 — PRICE ADVICE  (Ushauri wa Bei)
================================================================================
1. Click "Ushauri wa Bei" in the left menu.
2. Wait for the page to load.
3. Read the summary at the top ("Muhtasari wa biashara").
4. Scroll through the product list.
5. Find a product flagged "Hasara" (loss) or "Faida ndogo" (small profit).
6. Read its buying price, selling price, and proposed price
   ("Bei inayopendekezwa").
7. Click the refresh arrow at the top-right.
8. Wait for it to reload.


================================================================================
 WORKFLOW 2 — RESTOCK PLAN  (Mpango wa Kuagiza)
================================================================================
1. Click "Mpango wa Kuagiza" in the left menu.
2. Wait for the page to load.
3. Read the summary at the top ("Muhtasari wa kuagiza").
4. Scroll through the product list.
5. Find a product flagged "Imeisha" (out of stock) or "Stock ndogo" (low).
6. Read its stock, selling speed ("Mauzo kwa siku"), and days left
   ("Siku zilizosalia").
7. Read the "Agiza" box to see how many to order and the estimated cost.
8. Click the refresh arrow at the top-right.
9. Wait for it to reload.


================================================================================
 WORKFLOW 3 — ASK A QUESTION  (Uliza Biashara)
================================================================================
1. Click "Uliza Biashara" in the left menu.
2. Click one of the example questions under "Jaribu kuuliza"
   (e.g. "Niliuza mangapi wiki hii?").
3. Wait for the answer to appear.
4. Read the "Jibu" (answer), any table ("Maelezo") and any chart ("Chati").
5. Type your own question in the box (e.g. "Nilitumia kiasi gani kwenye
   usafiri?").
6. Press the send button ("Uliza").
7. Wait for the answer.
8. Ask one more follow-up question and wait for the answer.


================================================================================
 WORKFLOW 4 — BUSINESS HEALTH  (Afya ya Biashara)
================================================================================
1. Click "Afya ya Biashara" in the left menu.
2. Wait for the page to load.
3. Read the round score at the top ("Alama ya afya", out of 100).
4. Read the summary sentence and the period ("Kipindi").
5. Scroll down to "Vipimo" and read each bar.
6. Read "Mabadiliko muhimu" (what changed).
7. Read "Vitendo vinavyopendekezwa" (suggested actions).
8. Read "Historia ya wiki" (past weeks).
9. Click "Sasisha" at the top-right.
10. Wait for the new report to generate.


================================================================================
 PHONE APP (same four features as bottom tabs)
================================================================================
1. Open the Duka Mkononi app.
2. Log in as the Seller.
3. Tap the "Bei" tab    -> repeat WORKFLOW 1 steps.
4. Tap the "Agiza" tab  -> repeat WORKFLOW 2 steps.
5. Tap the "Uliza" tab  -> repeat WORKFLOW 3 steps.
6. Tap the "Afya" tab   -> repeat WORKFLOW 4 steps.
================================================================================
