ABOUT THIS GUIDE
----------------
This guide helps you check the new AI helpers in the Seller (Muuzaji) side of
Duka Mkononi. You do NOT need to understand any code. Just follow the steps and
watch what appears on the screen.

Everything here uses the normal Seller login. These AI helpers are for the
SHOP OWNER (Muuzaji), not for the admin. Wherever this guide says "AI", it means
the smart helper that explains your business in ordinary language.

IMPORTANT: The AI is only a helper. All the numbers (prices, profit, stock,
sales) come straight from your shop's real records. If the AI text is missing,
the numbers are still real and correct.


A QUICK NOTE ON HOW MANY FEATURES
---------------------------------
You mentioned 3 features. There are actually 4 ready to test. All four are for
the Seller account:

   1. PRICE ADVICE        (Swahili: Ushauri wa Bei)
   2. RESTOCK PLAN        (Swahili: Mpango wa Kuagiza)
   3. ASK A QUESTION      (Swahili: Uliza Biashara)
   4. BUSINESS HEALTH     (Swahili: Afya ya Biashara)

Feature 4 (Business Health) needs a one-time setup by your technical person
before its weekly history can be saved. You can still test and see it — details
are in TEST 4 below.

   -------------------+---------------------------------+------------------------------
   1 Price Advice     | Seller portal > "Ushauri wa Bei"| Shows which products are

   -------------------+---------------------------------+------------------------------

(On the phone app these are the last four tabs at the bottom: "Bei", "Agiza",
"Uliza", "Afya".)


WHAT YOU NEED BEFORE YOU START
------------------------------
- A Seller account that already has:
     * a few products with a BUYING price and a SELLING price,
     * at least some sales and one or two expenses,
     * ideally one product where the selling price is lower than the buying
       price (so you can see a warning).
- A working internet connection.
- Either a computer with a browser, or the Duka Mkononi phone app.


HOW TO LOG IN AND GET IN (on a computer / website)
--------------------------------------------------
   1. Open the Duka Mkononi website in your browser.
   2. Go to the Login page and sign in with the SELLER email and password.
   3. You will land on the Seller portal. On the left side there is a menu with
      items such as: Profaili, Mauzo, Matumizi, Uza, Oda.
   4. Scroll down in that menu. The four new items are at the bottom:
         - Ushauri wa Bei
         - Mpango wa Kuagiza
         - Uliza Biashara
         - Afya ya Biashara
   5. Use these menu items to move between the four features.

   (On the phone app: open the app, log in as the Seller, and the last four
    icons along the bottom are these same four features.)


================================================================================
 TEST 1 — PRICE ADVICE  (Ushauri wa Bei)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A list of your products that need attention because of their price, plus a
   suggested better selling price for each one. The suggested price is worked
   out precisely from your own buying/selling prices — the AI only explains it.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Ushauri wa Bei".
   2. Wait a few seconds for it to load ("Inachambua bei na faida...").
   3. Look at the summary at the top ("Muhtasari wa biashara").
   4. Scroll through the product list ("Bidhaa zinazohitaji uangalizi").
   5. Look at any product shown in red or orange, and read its label.
   6. Scroll to the bottom and read "Mambo ya kuangalia" and "Mipaka ya
      uchambuzi".
   7. Click the round refresh arrow at the top-right to reload.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - A summary with: number of products, number needing attention, and the
     average profit (Faida wastani).
   - Each product shows its buying price ("Bei ya kununua"), selling price
     ("Bei ya kuuzia"), profit ("Faida") and current stock ("Idadi iliyopo").
   - Some products are labelled in colour, for example:
         "Hasara" (Loss), "Faida ndogo" (Small profit), or "Nzuri" (Good).
   - For products that need attention, a green or blue box shows a
     "Bei inayopendekezwa" (suggested price) and a "Bei ya chini kabisa"
     (the lowest safe price).
   - At the top there is a small banner. If it says "Ushauri wa AI", the smart
     explanation is working. If it says the AI advice is unavailable, that is
     NOT a failure — the real numbers still show.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The page loaded and showed products.
   [ ] A product with a low price is clearly flagged (Loss / small profit).
   [ ] The suggested price is higher than the buying price for that product.
   [ ] Money amounts look sensible (e.g. "TZS 1,500").


================================================================================
 TEST 2 — RESTOCK PLAN  (Mpango wa Kuagiza)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A shopping list of what to re-order. It looks at how fast each product sells
   and how much is left, then suggests how many to buy. This is only a plan — it
   never changes your stock on its own.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Mpango wa Kuagiza".
   2. Wait for it to load ("Inachambua stock na mahitaji...").
   3. Look at the summary at the top ("Muhtasari wa kuagiza").
   4. Scroll through the product list ("Bidhaa zinazohitajika kuagizwa").
   5. Read the "Agiza" (order this many) box on any product that needs ordering.
   6. Read "Mambo ya kuangalia" and "Mipaka ya uchambuzi" at the bottom.
   7. Click the refresh arrow at the top-right to reload.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - A summary with: number of products, number to re-order, and number already
     finished ("Zimeisha").
   - Total suggested units and the estimated cost of re-ordering
     ("Gharama inayokadiriwa").
   - Each product shows: stock left ("Idadi iliyopo"), how fast it sells
     ("Mauzo kwa siku"), and days left ("Siku zilizosalia").
   - A product that is out of stock or low is clearly flagged, for example
     "Imeisha" (finished) or "Stock ndogo" (low stock).
   - Where it suggests an order, you see "Agiza" (how many), "Kiwango cha
     chini" (minimum you should keep) and "Gharama ya kuagiza" (cost).
   - Small labels may appear such as "Inaenda haraka" (fast-moving) or
     "Data chache" (little data).

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The page loaded and showed products.
   [ ] At least one product that is out of stock or low is flagged.
   [ ] The suggested order quantity and cost look sensible.
   [ ] Nothing in your stock changed just by opening this page.


================================================================================
 TEST 3 — ASK A QUESTION  (Uliza Biashara)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A question box where you type in normal language and it answers using your
   own shop's numbers. You can write in Swahili or English (or any of the
   supported languages).

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Uliza Biashara".
   2. Tap one of the ready-made example questions under "Jaribu kuuliza"
      (for example "Niliuza mangapi wiki hii?").
   3. Watch the answer appear ("Inatafuta jibu...").
   4. Now type your OWN question in the box and press the send button
      ("Uliza"). For example: "Nilitumia kiasi gani kwenye usafiri?"
   5. Read the "Jibu" (answer), any table ("Maelezo") and any chart ("Chati").
   6. Ask a second, related question to check it still understands.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - First, a line showing what it understood: the period ("Kipindi") and the
     kind of figure (Mauzo / Faida / Idadi).
   - A clear answer in your language, with your real figures.
   - Often a small table or a simple bar chart below the answer.
   - A green box called "Maoni ya AI" with a short comment. If this box is not
     there, the answer and numbers are still correct — not a failure.
   - If it does not understand the question, it says "Sikuelewa" instead of
     guessing.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] An example question gave a real answer with numbers.
   [ ] Your own typed question also gave a sensible answer.
   [ ] The numbers match what you expect from your recent sales.
   [ ] Asking "How many customers do I have?" gives a sensible reply.


================================================================================
 TEST 4 — BUSINESS HEALTH  (Afya ya Biashara)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A weekly report card for your shop: a score out of 100, what went well, what
   changed, and a few suggested actions for next week.

   NOTE FOR THE TECHNICAL PERSON: this feature needs a one-time setup (a small
   storage table) before the weekly history can be saved. Until that is done,
   the page will still open and show a score, but it will warn that the report
   was not saved and the history list will be empty. That warning alone is not
   a bug.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Afya ya Biashara".
   2. Wait for it to load ("Inatafuta ripoti...").
   3. Look at the big round score at the top ("Alama ya afya", out of 100).
   4. Read the summary text next to it, and the period ("Kipindi").
   5. Scroll down and read "Vipimo" (the parts that make up the score).
   6. Read "Mabadiliko muhimu" (what changed) and "Vitendo vinavyopendekezwa"
      (suggested actions).
   7. Read "Historia ya wiki" (past weeks).
   8. Click "Sasisha" (Refresh) at the top-right to create this week's report.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - A round score out of 100, coloured green / blue / orange / red.
   - A short sentence explaining the score, and the week it covers.
   - Bars under "Vipimo" with short explanations (for example profit, stock
     cover, spending).
   - Up/down arrows under "Mabadiliko muhimu" comparing with last week.
   - Two or three numbered suggested actions under "Vitendo vinavyopendekezwa".
   - A green "Maoni ya AI" box with extra comments.

   IMPORTANT: If your shop does not have enough sales or expense records, the
   page will honestly say "Data haitoshi" (not enough data) instead of giving a
   fake score. That is correct behaviour, not a failure.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The page loaded and showed a score (or an honest "not enough data" note).
   [ ] The score, bars and changes all look connected to your real activity.
   [ ] "Sasisha" created a fresh report.
   [ ] If you were warned that history was not saved, note it for the
       technical person (one-time setup still needed).


================================================================================
 EXTRA CHECKS (DO THESE ONCE, FOR ALL FOUR FEATURES)
================================================================================

LANGUAGE CHECK
   1. Find the language switcher on the page.
   2. Change the language to English and open each of the four features again.
   3. The titles, buttons and labels should be in English.
   4. Internet and the AI text should also follow the chosen language.
   [ ] All four features shown in the second language correctly.

NO-CONNECTION CHECK
   1. Turn off your internet (or aeroplane mode on the phone).
   2. Open one of the four features and press refresh.
   3. You should see a friendly message ("Hitilafu ya mtandao...").
   4. Turn the internet back on and refresh — it should load again.
   [ ] The app stayed usable and recovered after the connection returned.

SAFETY CHECK
   1. Notice that opening any of these pages does NOT change your stock, your
      sales or your expenses by itself. They only show information and advice.
   [ ] Nothing changed without you confirming it.

SECURITY CHECK (if possible, with two different shops)
   1. Log in as Seller A and note a product name and a sales figure.
   2. Log in as Seller B and open the same four features.
   3. Seller B must NEVER see Seller A's products, sales or numbers.
   [ ] Each seller only sees their own shop's information.

   - Which feature were you in? (Price / Restock / Ask / Health)

   - What did you expect to see?
   - What actually happened? (copy the message on the screen)
   - A screenshot or photo of the screen, if you can.
   - The seller account you were using.
   - The time and date it happened.
