ABOUT THIS GUIDE
----------------
This guide helps you check the new AI helpers in Duka Mkononi. Most of them
are in the Seller (Muuzaji) side; two small extra checks use the Admin and
Customer accounts (TEST 7 and TEST 8). You do NOT need to understand any code.
Just follow the steps and watch what appears on the screen.

Everything here uses the normal login for the account named in each test.
Wherever this guide says "AI", it means the smart helper that explains your
business in ordinary language.

IMPORTANT: The AI is only a helper. All the numbers (prices, profit, stock,
sales) come straight from your shop's real records. If the AI text is missing,
the numbers are still real and correct.

WHAT YOU NEED BEFORE YOU START
------------------------------
- A Seller account that already has:
     * a few products with a BUYING price and a SELLING price,
     * at least some sales and one or two expenses,
     * ideally one product where the selling price is lower than the buying
       price (so you can see a warning).
- A working internet connection.
- Either a computer with a browser, or the Duka Mkononi phone app.
- For the Ad Studio (TEST 6): at least one product that already has a photo.
- For TEST 11 (Shopping Assistant): nothing extra — the Shop page works
  without logging in, and the Customer app just needs the "Tafuta" tab.
- For TEST 13 (Recommendations): log in as a Customer who has ordered at
  least once to see personalised reasons; signed out you get the honest
  cold-start feed instead (popular + certified items).
- For TEST 14 (Report Summary): log in as the shop's Msimamizi (admin) or
  Muuzaji account on the Reports page, and make sure the shop has some
  recorded sales and expenses - the summary explains your real numbers.


HOW TO LOG IN AND GET IN (on a computer / website)
--------------------------------------------------
   1. Open the Duka Mkononi website in your browser.
   2. Go to the Login page and sign in with the SELLER email and password.
   3. You will land on the Seller portal. On the left side there is a menu with
      items such as: Profaili, Mauzo, Matumizi, Uza, Oda.
   4. Click "Huduma Nyingine" (the last menu item). It is the HUB that opens
      all the AI helpers as cards:
         - Ushauri wa Bei
         - Mpango wa Kuagiza
         - Uliza Biashara
         - Afya ya Biashara
         - Tangaza Bidhaa        (NEW — the Ad Studio)
      The AI expense helper (TEST 5) is NOT here — it lives inside "Matumizi".
   5. Use these cards to move between the features.

   (On the phone app: open the app and log in as the Seller. All five hub
    cards open from the "🧩" (Nyingine) tab; the AI expense helper is inside
    the "Matumizi" tab.)

 TEST 1 — PRICE ADVICE  (Ushauri wa Bei)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A list of your products that need attention because of their price, plus a
   suggested better selling price for each one. The suggested price is worked
   out precisely from your own buying/selling prices — the AI only explains it.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Huduma Nyingine", then click the card
      "Ushauri wa Bei".
   2. Wait a few seconds for it to load ("Inachambua bei na faida...").
   3. Look at the summary at the top ("Muhtasari wa biashara").
   4. Scroll through the product list ("Bidhaa zinazohitaji uangalizi").
   5. Look at any product shown in red or orange, and read its label.
   6. Scroll to the bottom and read "Mambo ya kuangalia" and "Mipaka ya
      uchambuzi".
   7. Click the round refresh arrow at the top-right to reload.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - A summary with: number of products, number needing attention, and the
     average profit (Faida wastani).
   - Each product shows its buying price ("Bei ya kununua"), selling price
     ("Bei ya kuuzia"), profit ("Faida") and current stock ("Idadi iliyopo").
   - Some products are labelled in colour, for example:
         "Hasara" (Loss), "Faida ndogo" (Small profit), or "Nzuri" (Good).
   - For products that need attention, a green or blue box shows a
     "Bei inayopendekezwa" (suggested price) and a "Bei ya chini kabisa"
     (the lowest safe price).
   - At the top there is a small banner. If it says "Ushauri wa AI", the smart
     explanation is working. If it says the AI advice is unavailable, that is
     NOT a failure — the real numbers still show.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The page loaded and showed products.
   [ ] A product with a low price is clearly flagged (Loss / small profit).
   [ ] The suggested price is higher than the buying price for that product.
   [ ] Money amounts look sensible (e.g. "TZS 1,500").

 TEST 2 — RESTOCK PLAN  (Mpango wa Kuagiza)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A shopping list of what to re-order. It looks at how fast each product sells
   and how much is left, then suggests how many to buy. This is only a plan — it
   never changes your stock on its own.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Huduma Nyingine", then click the card
      "Mpango wa Kuagiza".
   2. Wait for it to load ("Inachambua stock na mahitaji...").
   3. Look at the summary at the top ("Muhtasari wa kuagiza").
   4. Scroll through the product list ("Bidhaa zinazohitajika kuagizwa").
   5. Read the "Agiza" (order this many) box on any product that needs ordering.
   6. Read "Mambo ya kuangalia" and "Mipaka ya uchambuzi" at the bottom.
   7. Click the refresh arrow at the top-right to reload.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - A summary with: number of products, number to re-order, and number already
     finished ("Zimeisha").
   - Total suggested units and the estimated cost of re-ordering
     ("Gharama inayokadiriwa").
   - Each product shows: stock left ("Idadi iliyopo"), how fast it sells
     ("Mauzo kwa siku"), and days left ("Siku zilizosalia").
   - A product that is out of stock or low is clearly flagged, for example
     "Imeisha" (finished) or "Stock ndogo" (low stock).
   - Where it suggests an order, you see "Agiza" (how many), "Kiwango cha
     chini" (minimum you should keep) and "Gharama ya kuagiza" (cost).
   - Small labels may appear such as "Inaenda haraka" (fast-moving) or
     "Data chache" (little data).

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The page loaded and showed products.
   [ ] At least one product that is out of stock or low is flagged.
   [ ] The suggested order quantity and cost look sensible.
   [ ] Nothing in your stock changed just by opening this page.

 TEST 3 — ASK A QUESTION  (Uliza Biashara)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A question box where you type in normal language and it answers using your
   own shop's numbers. You can write in Swahili or English (or any of the
   supported languages).

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Huduma Nyingine", then click the card
      "Uliza Biashara".
   2. Tap one of the ready-made example questions under "Jaribu kuuliza"
      (for example "Niliuza mangapi wiki hii?").
   3. Watch the answer appear ("Inatafuta jibu...").
   4. Now type your OWN question in the box and press the send button
      ("Uliza"). For example: "Nilitumia kiasi gani kwenye usafiri?"
   5. Read the "Jibu" (answer), any table ("Maelezo") and any chart ("Chati").
   6. Ask a second, related question to check it still understands.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - First, a line showing what it understood: the period ("Kipindi") and the
     kind of figure (Mauzo / Faida / Idadi).
   - A clear answer in your language, with your real figures.
   - Often a small table or a simple bar chart below the answer.
   - A green box called "Maoni ya AI" with a short comment. If this box is not
     there, the answer and numbers are still correct — not a failure.
   - If it does not understand the question, it says "Sikuelewa" instead of
     guessing.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] An example question gave a real answer with numbers.
   [ ] Your own typed question also gave a sensible answer.
   [ ] The numbers match what you expect from your recent sales.
   [ ] Asking "How many customers do I have?" gives a sensible reply.

 TEST 4 — BUSINESS HEALTH  (Afya ya Biashara)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A weekly report card for your shop: a score out of 100, what went well, what
   changed, and a few suggested actions for next week.

   NOTE FOR THE TECHNICAL PERSON: this feature needs a one-time setup (a small
   storage table) before the weekly history can be saved. Until that is done,
   the page will still open and show a score, but it will warn that the report
   was not saved and the history list will be empty. That warning alone is not
   a bug.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Huduma Nyingine", then click the card
      "Afya ya Biashara".
   2. Wait for it to load ("Inatafuta ripoti...").
   3. Look at the big round score at the top ("Alama ya afya", out of 100).
   4. Read the summary text next to it, and the period ("Kipindi").
   5. Scroll down and read "Vipimo" (the parts that make up the score).
   6. Read "Mabadiliko muhimu" (what changed) and "Vitendo vinavyopendekezwa"
      (suggested actions).
   7. Read "Historia ya wiki" (past weeks).
   8. Click "Sasisha" (Refresh) at the top-right to create this week's report.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - A round score out of 100, coloured green / blue / orange / red.
   - A short sentence explaining the score, and the week it covers.
   - Bars under "Vipimo" with short explanations (for example profit, stock
     cover, spending).
   - Up/down arrows under "Mabadiliko muhimu" comparing with last week.
   - Two or three numbered suggested actions under "Vitendo vinavyopendekezwa".
   - A green "Maoni ya AI" box with extra comments.

   IMPORTANT: If your shop does not have enough sales or expense records, the
   page will honestly say "Data haitoshi" (not enough data) instead of giving a
   fake score. That is correct behaviour, not a failure.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The page loaded and showed a score (or an honest "not enough data" note).
   [ ] The score, bars and changes all look connected to your real activity.
   [ ] "Sasisha" created a fresh report.
   [ ] If you were warned that history was not saved, note it for the
       technical person (one-time setup still needed).

 TEST 5 — AI EXPENSE ENTRY  (inside "Matumizi")
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   Instead of typing every field of an expense by hand, you write one short
   sentence — like a note to yourself — and AI fills in the amount,
   description and category for you. YOU still check everything and press the
   normal Save button; nothing is saved by the AI itself.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Matumizi".
   2. Start adding an expense (the add-expense form).
   3. Find the box labelled "Andika matumizi kwa maneno" (type expenses in
      words), with the example "Nauli ya usafiri 5000 jana".
   4. Type something similar, for example: "Maji 3000 jana".
   5. Click the button "Chambua kwa AI" (Analyse with AI).
   6. Wait for the message "Inachambua maelezo..." and then the note
      "Taarifa zimewekwa kwenye fomu - hakikisha kabla ya kuhifadhi."
   7. Check the filled-in amount, description and category. Change anything
      that looks wrong.
   8. Press the normal Save button to store the expense.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - The amount, description and category are filled in from your sentence,
     and you can still edit every field by hand.
   - If your sentence had NO amount, the app asks you to type it — it never
     invents a number.
   - If you wrote two possible amounts ("5000 au 7000"), the first is used
     and a warning asks you to check it.
   - If the same expense was saved very recently, a warning says so
     ("Onyo: matumizi kama haya yamehifadhiwa hivi karibuni...").
   - If the category is not certain, you are asked to pick one from the list.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The form was filled in from my sentence and I could edit it.
   [ ] No amount appeared that I did not type.
   [ ] Saving worked exactly like before (same expense list and totals).
   [ ] The warning lines appeared when something needed checking.

 TEST 6 — AD STUDIO  (Swahili: Tangaza Bidhaa)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A small studio that writes advertising text for ONE of your products — a
   headline and a description — using your product's real price, stock and
   description. You choose the language, tone, length and purpose. The text is
   only a draft: you edit it, save it as a draft, and publish it only when YOU
   press the publish button (which uses the normal paid advertising flow).

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Huduma Nyingine".
   2. Click the red card "Tangaza Bidhaa" (Promote Products).
   3. Under "1. Chagua bidhaa" pick one product.
   4. Under "2. Vidhibiti vya nakala" choose the content type, tone and
      length; optionally fill in audience, language and objective
      (e.g. "mauzo ya mwezi").
   5. Click "Tengeneza nakla" (Generate copy) and wait.
   6. Under "3. Hakikisha na hariri" read the draft and EDIT it freely —
      the text boxes are yours.
   7. Optional: click "Hifadhi rasimu" (Save draft) and find it later under
      "Rasimu zilizohifadhiwa".
   8. When you are happy, click "Chapisha..." (Publish) and confirm.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - The product's name appears in the text exactly as it is in your product
     list (if it is missing, a warning line tells you to add it).
   - Prices in the text match your real prices — the helper may not invent a
     price or a discount. If the draft broke that rule it is refused and you
     are asked to generate again.
   - Amber warning lines appear for: text shortened to fit the limit, missing
     product name, or a product that is out of stock.
   - Character counters show the text inside the allowed length.
   - Publishing opens a confirmation, then the normal advertisement payment
     (TZS 3,000). IMPORTANT: the advertisement only goes LIVE after the
     payment completes — cancel the payment and it simply stays inactive.
   - With no products, the page says "Hakuna bidhaa" and offers "Nenda Kuuza".

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] A draft was generated in the language I chose, for the product I chose.
   [ ] I could edit the text before doing anything else.
   [ ] My draft appeared under "Rasimu zilizohifadhiwa" after saving it.
   [ ] Nothing was published until I pressed "Chapisha..." and confirmed.
   [ ] (If you paid) the advertisement now appears in the public list;
       (if you cancelled) it does NOT.

 TEST 7 — ANOMALY WATCH  (Admin account, "Nyingine")
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A watch-list for the ADMIN (Msimamizi) portal. It checks the shop's sales,
   spending and stock with fixed rules and lists anything suspicious — for
   example a sale recorded below the buying price, near-identical sales saved
   twice, or spending that jumped. It only SHOWS warnings; it never changes
   any records, and you can mark one as checked-and-fine.

STEPS
   1. Log in to the website with the ADMIN account.
   2. Open the "Nyingine" menu item (phone app: the "Nyingine" tab).
   3. Wait for it to load ("Inapakua dalili...").
   4. Read the warnings: each has a title, a severity level (Juu/Kati/Chini),
      the real figures under "Ushahidi" (evidence) and a "Thibitisha" (verify)
      note.
   5. If something is actually fine, click "Nimeukagua" (I have checked it)
      and confirm — the warning leaves the watch-list.
   6. The list reloads by itself; the warning now appears under "Kumbukumbu
      za ukaguzi" (inspection records).

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - Warnings only appear when your data really contains the pattern; a clean
     shop shows an empty list — that is a good result, not a bug.
   - Each warning shows the real figures involved (amounts, counts).
   - The same problem keeps the same warning after refreshing (it does not
     multiply).
   - The AI summary box may say it is unavailable — the warnings themselves
     still appear, because the checks are ordinary code, not AI.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The page opened and listed warnings (or honestly showed none).
   [ ] Dismissing a warning removed it from the open list and kept it in the
       history.
   [ ] No sales, expenses or stock changed just by opening the page.

 TEST 8 — ORDER QUESTIONS  (Customer account, "Nyingine")
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A question box for the CUSTOMER (Mteja) portal: ask about YOUR OWN order
   in normal words and get an answer built from what the shop actually
   recorded — never a guess.

STEPS
   1. Log in to the website (or app) with a CUSTOMER account that has an order.
   2. Open the "Nyingine" menu item / tab — the page is called
      "Maswali ya Oda" (Order Questions).
   3. Type a question such as "Oda yangu iko wapi?" (Where is my order?) or
      tap the example "Mfano: Oda yangu iko wapi?".
   4. Press the ask button and read the answer.
   5. If you know your order number (DM-...), include it in the question.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - An answer in your language that quotes your order's real reference and
     recorded status, plus a list explaining what each status means.
   - A wrong/unknown order number gets an honest "not found" reply instead of
     a made-up one.
   - An account with no orders (or no phone number) gets an honest explanation
     — that is correct behaviour, not a failure.
   - The answer never promises a delivery date that the shop has not recorded.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] My question about my own order was answered with real details.
   [ ] I could not get information about an order that is not mine.
   [ ] The "limitations" note at the bottom was shown.

 TEST 9 — REPLY DRAFTING  (Seller account, inside "Oda")
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   When a customer writes a question into their order (for example "Where is
   my order?"), this assistant drafts the seller's answer using only what the
   order really records - the items, the real prices and the current status.
   You pick a friendly or formal tone, the draft appears in the note box, you
   edit it, and it is only sent when YOU press the normal Save button. It
   never invents delivery times, discounts or refunds.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Oda" (phone app: the "Oda" bottom tab).
   2. Open an order whose note contains a customer question (View).
   3. Under the question, pick the tone: "Rafiki" (friendly) or "Rasmi"
      (formal).
   4. Click "Tengeneza jibu (AI)" (Draft reply with AI) and wait.
   5. Read the draft inside the customer-note box. Edit it freely.
   6. Read any amber lines under the button - they warn about removed
      promises (delivery time, discount), a possible duplicate, or missing
      information you must add yourself.
   7. Press the normal Save button to send it, exactly like a typed note.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - The draft is in the customer's language and answers the question that
     was actually asked on this order.
   - Amounts in the draft always match this order - a wrong amount is
     refused and you are asked to generate again.
   - The draft never promises a delivery day, a discount or a refund that is
     not already recorded in the order's notes; such sentences are removed
     and flagged.
   - While the order is not yet delivered and paid, amber lines remind you
     about the missing timing / payment - the assistant will not claim them.
   - Sending the exact same note twice asks for confirmation first.
   - Nothing is sent by itself: the note leaves only when you press Save.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] A draft was created for the order's question and I could edit it.
   [ ] The reply quoted only amounts that exist on this order.
   [ ] The amber warnings appeared where information was missing.
   [ ] The duplicate confirmation appeared when I re-sent an identical note.
   [ ] The customer received the note only after I pressed Save.

 TEST 10 — BUSINESS ASSISTANT  (Msaidizi wa Biashara)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A chat assistant for questions about your own shop. You ask in normal
   words - about sales, profit, stock, spending or the health of the
   business - and it answers using your real records. It remembers what you
   asked earlier in the same conversation (for example "make it weekly" or
   "and last month?"). It only READS your data: nothing in your shop changes
   by asking a question, and it never invents your numbers.

STEPS
   1. In the Seller menu, click "Huduma Nyingine", then click the card
      "Msaidizi wa Biashara" (phone app: the "🧩" Nyingine hub tab).
   2. Tap one of the ready-made example questions under "Jaribu kuuliza",
      for example "Niliuza mangapi wiki hii?" (How much did I sell this
      week?).
   3. Read the answer. Look at the small badge naming the part of your data
      that was used (Mauzo / Bidhaa / Matumizi / Wateja / Afya) and the
      "Vyanzo" (sources) line under it.
   4. Ask a FOLLOW-UP question about the same topic, for example "Na ya
      mwezi jana?" (And last month's?). It should still understand you are
      talking about sales.
   5. Try a stock question: "Niko na bidhaa gani zinakaribia kuisha?" (Which
      products are running out?).
   6. Try a health question: "Biashara yangu iko aje?" (How is my business?).
   7. Ask something that is NOT about your business, for example "Nani ni
      rais wa Tanzania?". It should honestly say it cannot help with that
      instead of guessing.
   8. On the left you see "Mazungumzo yako" (your conversations). Click an

 TEST 12 — TRUST SUMMARY  (Hali ya Kuaminika)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A "✨" button on each business card that tells you what the PLATFORM
   knows about that shop, kept strictly separate from what CUSTOMERS say:

   - "Ukweli uliothibitishwa" (Verified facts): certification state and
     date, how many products are on sale, and order figures - all taken
     from the system's own records.
   - "Maoni ya wateja" (Customer opinions): how many published reviews
     there are, their average score, the period they cover, and which words
     keep coming up (said fairly: the same word praised in one review and
     complained about in another is shown as "mseto" / mixed).
   - "Maoni asilia" (Original reviews): the actual published reviews with
     name, stars, date and text, plus a link to all reviews.

   The two blocks are never mixed: an opinion can never appear as a fact.
   Reviews on this platform are about the platform as a whole, and the
   summary says so. Nothing private (e-mail, phone number, unpublished
   notes) is ever shown, and nothing is invented - with no reviews the
   summary honestly says there are none.

STEPS (website)
   1. Open "Biashara" (Businesses) from the website menu - no login needed.
   2. On any business card, press "✨ Hali ya Kuaminika" under "Onyesha
      bidhaa".
   3. Read the summary line, then the two blocks: verified facts first,
      customer opinions second. Note the sample size on the opinion lines
      ("Maoni N yaliyochapishwa..."), not just a score.
   4. Scroll to the original reviews and press "Onyesha maoni yote" to see
      all of them on the home page.
   5. Press the button again to close the panel; press once more to reopen.
   6. Change the language and open the panel again - labels and summary
      follow the language.

STEPS (phone app)
   1. Log in as Customer and open the "Biashara" tab.
   2. Press "✨ Hali ya Kuaminika" on any business card - the panel opens
      right inside the card (one at a time).

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - Facts and opinions in visibly separate labelled blocks.
   - Opinion lines that always say how many reviews they are based on and
     over which dates.
   - Themes (recurring words) with a fairness label: chanya / hasi / mseto
     (positive / negative / mixed).
   - A note that reviews are for the platform as a whole, and a note about
     how long the summary is cached (15 minutes by default).
   - With no reviews: "Hakuna maoni yaliyochapishwa bado" plus the shop's
     verified facts - never a made-up rating.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] Facts show the real certification state, product count and order
       numbers of that business.
   [ ] Opinions show the real number of published reviews (same as the
       reviews on the home page) and their dates.
   [ ] An opinion never appeared inside the facts block.
   [ ] A word praised and complained about was shown as "mseto" (mixed),
       not flattened to one side.
   [ ] Original reviews were readable with name/stars/date and the link
       opened all reviews.
   [ ] With no published reviews it said so instead of inventing any.
   [ ] Closing and reopening (or leaving the tab) still showed the summary.

 TEST 15 — CONTENT REVIEW  (Ukaguzi wa Maudhui)
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
   A "✨ Ukaguzi wa Maudhui" (Content Review) card that reads your own
   advertisements and product listings and shows what is wrong with them
   BEFORE a customer does: incomplete information, unsupported claims,
   misleading prices and prohibited content.

   - The findings come from fixed rules the server runs itself. Each one
     shows the exact text that triggered it, next to a severity chip:
     red "Kukiukwa" (definite violation) or orange "Mambo ya kuangalia"
     (uncertain concern).
   - When a finding involves a claim, a small AI note may add
     "supported / uncertain / unsupported" beside it. It is clearly
     marked as advice, can never add or remove a finding, and never
     decides anything.
   - Nothing is approved or rejected by the AI: YOU press "Idhini"
     (Approve), "Kataa" (Reject) or "Omba Marekebisho" (Request edits),
     tick "Nimesoma mabao yote" (I have read all the panels), add a
     reason and press the button.
   - Approve switches the item ON, Reject switches it OFF, Request edits
     leaves it unchanged. The decision and its reason are recorded in
     the audit trail (AI_REVIEW_DECISION).

STEPS (website)
   1. Log in as Msimamizi (or Muuzaji) and open "Tangaza" (Adverts).
   2. On Msimamizi, open the "Matangazo yangu" tab; on Muuzaji the
      "✨ Ukaguzi wa Maudhui" card sits directly under the page header.
      Press "Kagua Sasa" (Check Now).
   3. Read the four counts (Vilivyokaguliwa / Kukiukwa / Mambo ya
      kuangalia / Safi), then switch between the "Matangazo" and
      "Bidhaa" tabs of the card.
   4. Pick a decision on one item, tick "Nimesoma mabao yote", type a
      reason and press the decision button.
   5. Press "Kagua Sasa" again: the item's active/inactive badge must
      now follow your decision.

STEPS (phone app)
   1. Msimamizi or Muuzaji login -> "Tangaza": the same card sits above
      the ads (Msimamizi: on the "Matangazo yangu" tab).
   2. Press "Kagua Sasa", pick a decision, tick the box, press the
      button - a confirmation dialog reports "Uamuzi umerekodiwa."

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   - Every excerpt quoted under a finding is real text from that exact
     ad or product.
   - A green "✓" when an item has no findings.
   - Rejecting an item that has a definite violation WITHOUT a reason
     is refused with a message - the reason is mandatory.
   - Submitting without ticking "Nimesoma mabao yote" is refused.
   - The AI note (when present) sits NEXT TO a finding and never
     changes its severity or your decision.

CHECK WHEN DONE
   [ ] The counts on the card match the findings in the lists below.
   [ ] Every excerpt shown is real text from that ad/product.
   [ ] Approve turned the item on; Reject turned it off; Request edits
       changed nothing.
   [ ] Rejecting a violation without a reason was refused.
   [ ] The "I have read" box was required before saving.
   [ ] Opening a language other than Swahili translated the whole card.
   [ ] Nothing was published or removed without you pressing a button.
