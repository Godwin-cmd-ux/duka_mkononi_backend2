* Product name.
* Selling price.
* Relevant availability information.
* Business name.
* Order / View Details button.

## 8. Track Orders Page

Create a customer-facing Track Orders page.

The customer must be able to retrieve their orders using the phone number supplied during shopping.

### Customer flow

1. The customer opens Track Orders.
2. The customer enters the phone number used during ordering.
3. The customer submits the form.
4. The system validates the phone number.
5. The system retrieves matching orders and displays the permitted information.

Show each order with:

* Order reference.
* Order date.
* Relevant ordered products.
* Quantities.
* Total amount.
* Current order status.
* Order progress or milestone information, where supported.
* Seller's latest customer-visible order note.
* Relevant status update dates, where available.

Customers should be able to view the orders they have placed using the supplied phone number.

### Privacy and security

A phone number alone is not a strong authentication mechanism. Do not expose sensitive information or allow unrestricted access to internal administrative notes.

At a minimum:

* Apply rate limiting to tracking requests.
* Normalize phone numbers consistently.
* Return only the information necessary to track an order.
* Exclude private staff notes, payment secrets, internal identifiers, and other sensitive information.
* Avoid exposing sequential internal database IDs.
* Prevent enumeration attacks and excessive repeated lookups.
* Use a suitable verification mechanism, such as an order reference or OTP, if needed to protect detailed order information.

Preserve the requested phone-number lookup experience while implementing appropriate safeguards.

### Order notes

Sellers may maintain internal notes and customer-visible notes separately if necessary.

Only customer-visible notes may appear on the public Track Orders page.
